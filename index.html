Drivanta Agro Foods is a family-driven enterprise based in the rich agricultural belt of Nandurbar, Maharashtra. We specialize in producing premium dehydrated vegetables and fruits — sourced directly from local farms and processed using modern dehydration techniques that preserve nutrition, flavor, and color without any chemicals or preservatives.

Our mission is to bring farm-fresh purity directly to households, restaurants, food manufacturers, and exporters across India — with the guarantee of zero compromises on quality.

— Mohit Patil, Founder, Drivanta Agro Foods
